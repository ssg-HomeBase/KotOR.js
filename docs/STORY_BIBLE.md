# KOTOR III (fan) story bible: Phase 1

A non-commercial fan project that runs as a mod on top of each player's own copy of KOTOR II. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Lucasfilm, Disney, Obsidian, BioWare or Aspyr. All writing here is original; no game dialogue is quoted.

How to read this file:
- **Lore** lines are facts from published Star Wars sources, paraphrased, each followed by its source URL and the date it was read. Unless marked otherwise, they are **Star Wars Legends** continuity, which Wookieepedia files KOTOR II under (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_II:_The_Sith_Lords, seen 2026-10-24).
- **Fan invention** lines are this project's own story choices. They are not lore and need no source.
- Wookieepedia article pages block automated readers, so their text was read through the Fandom API (`https://starwars.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=<Title>&prop=wikitext&format=json`, HTTP 200). Each citation gives the normal article URL.

## 1. What KOTOR II's ending sets up (lore)

1. In the light-side ending later treated as canon for Legends, the Exile defeats Darth Sion and then Kreia (as Darth Traya) on Malachor V. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_II:_The_Sith_Lords, seen 2026-10-24)
2. As she dies, Kreia foresees what will become of the companions and the worlds the Exile visited, and reveals that Revan went into the Unknown Regions after finding evidence of the "True Sith". (same URL, and https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreia, seen 2026-10-24)
3. Kreia tells the Exile that the companions she trained are the "Lost Jedi", around whom a new Jedi Order will be rebuilt. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Kreia, seen 2026-10-24)
4. Malachor V collapses, the companions fly the damaged *Ebon Hawk* out and pick up the Exile, and the game ends with her heading into the Unknown Regions after Revan. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ebon_Hawk, seen 2026-10-24)
5. Before the finale, Admiral Carth Onasi privately asks the Exile to help find Revan. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_II:_The_Sith_Lords, seen 2026-10-24)
6. Legends sources later fixed the Exile as female with the light-side ending, and the 2011 novel *Revan* named her Meetra Surik. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meetra_Surik, seen 2026-10-24)

## 2. What later Legends works say happened next (lore)

7. In *The Old Republic: Revan* (Drew Karpyshyn, 2011), Revan travels to the dead world Nathema, where he is captured by Sith; T3-M4 repairs the *Ebon Hawk* and returns to Republic space. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/The_Old_Republic:_Revan, seen 2026-10-24)
8. After the events of KOTOR II, Meetra Surik takes T3-M4's recording of Revan's capture, follows his trail to Nathema and then to Dromund Kaas, the hidden capital of the Sith Empire. (same URL, and https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meetra_Surik, seen 2026-10-24)
9. On Dromund Kaas she allies with Lord Scourge and frees Revan, who has been held for three years; their attack on the Emperor fails, Scourge kills Surik, T3-M4 is destroyed, and Revan is kept in stasis, where he quietly slows the Emperor's invasion plans. Nobody is left to claim the *Ebon Hawk*. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meetra_Surik and https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Ebon_Hawk, seen 2026-10-24)
10. Surik's trained Jedi go on to help rebuild the Order, and her spirit stays with the imprisoned Revan. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Meetra_Surik, seen 2026-10-24)
11. The Sith Emperor, once called Vitiate, drained a whole world (Nathema) in a ritual and led the surviving Sith to Dromund Kaas. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Vitiate, seen 2026-10-24)
12. The "True Sith" Empire was founded by Sith exiles who fled into the Unknown Regions after the Great Hyperspace War, settled Dromund Kaas, and rebuilt in secret for over a thousand years before invading the Republic roughly three centuries after Revan's time (the Great Galactic War of *Star Wars: The Old Republic*). (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/True_Sith and https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Dromund_Kaas/Legends, seen 2026-10-24)
13. In current Disney canon none of this exists; Revan survives only as an ancient Sith Lord whose name Sith Eternal cultists chant and whose name the Sith Eternal "Revan Legion" carries. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Revan, seen 2026-10-24)

## 3. What the original developers planned (real world)

14. Chris Avellone said a third KOTOR was in pre-production at Obsidian, would have featured Revan more and had the player, as the Exile, track him down; LucasArts never moved it forward. (https://www.engadget.com/2013-08-01-knights-of-the-old-republic-3-was-in-pre-production-at-obsidian.html, seen 2026-10-24)
15. In 2019 Avellone said the series was meant as a trilogy, with KOTOR 3 following Revan's trail to ancient Sith Lords far more powerful than the familiar ones. (https://steamcommunity.com/news/post/3763229740650005667, PC Gamer via Steam News, seen 2026-10-24)
16. LucasArts confirmed a KOTOR follow-up in 2007, but no KOTOR III was released; BioWare's MMO *The Old Republic* was announced in 2008. (https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Knights_of_the_Old_Republic_III, seen 2026-10-24)
17. Fans have long imagined their own KOTOR III; Deadlystream thread 7341 (2019) is one solo concept built on KOTOR II; its posts, over three pages, run from 30 August to 26 September 2019, and no release was found. (https://deadlystream.com/topic/7341-knights-of-the-old-republic-iii, seen 2026-10-24)

Design consequence (fan invention): to stay consistent with lore 9 to 12, this sequel does not defeat the Emperor, rescue Revan or prevent the Great Galactic War. It is about what the Exile left behind and what one crew can do with it.

## 4. Recommended premise: "The Last Signal" (fan invention)

**Logline.** Three years after the Exile vanished beyond the star charts, a courier on Citadel Station is still holding the last recording she sent home. It is sealed with the Force, and it opens for you.

**Who you play.** A new character the player creates: a Force-sensitive who grew up on Citadel Station during the Telos restoration and has never been trained. Class and appearance use KOTOR II's own character creation.

**What the recording says.** The Exile found the empire Kreia warned of (lore 2), learned where it hides, and asks whoever opens the seal to warn the Lost Jedi (lore 3) and the Republic. The recording ends with coordinates and static.

**What the story is about.** Legacy and cost. The player follows the Exile's route into the Unknown Regions, learns what happened to her (lore 9), and must decide what to carry home: a warning, a weapon, or a deal.

**Where it sits.** Set about three years after the Exile left (fan invention, timed to match lore 9's three years of Revan's captivity). Legends events stay true; the player's actions fill gaps the sources leave open.

### Cast (fan invention, except where marked)

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| Seren Vaux | Courier on Citadel Station who kept the Exile's recording. Opens the story (the Phase 1 scene); becomes the crew's pilot. |
| Admiral Carth Onasi (returning character; see lore 5) | Patron. Quietly funds the expedition because he once asked the Exile to find Revan. Appears by hologram; no new voice lines are reused from the games. |
| Tamsin Rho | Jedi apprentice trained by one of the Lost Jedi (lore 3, 10). Teaches the player and doubts the mission. |
| Rhea Vex | Mandalorian scout sent by the clans to learn where Revan went. Pragmatic, funny, loyal to the clans first. |
| VX-9 | Salvage droid that recovered the drone carrying the recording. Holds fragments of the Exile's flight logs; unlocks story by stages. |
| Ashkar | Young True Sith acolyte who defects. Light path: an ally who wants out. Dark path: the player's apprentice. |
| Darth Severis | Antagonist. A Dark Council lord who leads the Empire's hidden scouts in Republic space and hunts anyone who learns of Dromund Kaas. |
| The Sith Emperor (lore 11) | Never seen. His presence is felt through Severis and through Revan's prison. |

### Companions

Seren Vaux, Tamsin Rho, Rhea Vex, VX-9 and Ashkar join the party (five companions, to keep writing and voice scope realistic for a fan team).

### Act structure

- **Prologue: The Message (Citadel Station).** Seren stops the player on the docks and offers the recording (Phase 1 scene). The player chooses whether to hear her out.
- **Act I: The Signal (Telos, Nar Shaddaa, Dantooine).** Break the seal with help from the Lost Jedi, find a navigator and a ship, and survive Severis's first attempt to bury the secret. Recruit Seren, Tamsin, Rhea and VX-9.
- **Act II: Beyond the Charts (Nathema, the graveyard).** Follow the Exile's route. On Nathema, learn what the Emperor did to a world (lore 11). Capture or befriend Ashkar. VX-9's logs reveal that the Exile reached Dromund Kaas.
- **Act III: Dromund Kaas.** Infiltrate the outskirts of Kaas City, learn how the Exile died (lore 9) and find Revan beyond reach in his prison. The player cannot free him; the choice is what to take home.
- **Epilogue.** Light: the warning reaches the Lost Jedi and the Republic, who start a quiet watch on the Unknown Regions. Dark: the player trades the coordinates to the Empire for power. Either way, the war history records comes three centuries later (lore 12).

### The opening scene (built in Phase 1)

Where: Citadel Station docks on Telos (KOTOR II area `201tel`, loaded from the player's own game). Who: Seren Vaux. Length: 14 dialogue nodes (7 lines for Seren, 7 for the player), with two choices.

1. Seren stops the player: everyone else walks past, but the player slowed down, and she has carried a message for three years that she thinks is meant for them.
2. **Choice:** ask who sent it, or say they are not interested. Not interested leads to her parting line: the message will wait, but whatever is out there will not.
3. She explains: a ship left Telos after the Sith Lords fell, carrying a Jedi past every star chart after someone who went first. The player names the Exile.
4. She says no one on the station says that name anymore; the recording came back on a drone with coordinates and a warning.
5. The warning: the Sith they fought were the outer edge of something older and patient, waiting beyond the charts and almost ready to come home.
6. Why the player: the recording is sealed with the Force, and the seal lit up in her hands as the player walked by.
7. **Choice:** go now (she says to meet her at the docking ring with a ship and a crew that will not turn back) or take time to think (she gives her parting line).

The full text is in `mods/kotor3/src/k3_seren.dialogue.json`.

## 5. Alternate premises (one line each, fan invention)

- **The Exile's Road:** play as the Exile herself from Malachor V to Dromund Kaas, a tragedy whose ending players know is coming (lore 8, 9).
- **The Lost Jedi:** play as one of the Exile's trained companions rebuilding the Jedi Order on Dantooine while a True Sith vanguard hunts them in Republic space (lore 3, 10).
